Mollie Subscription Renewals Dashboard & Recurring Payments

Automate and Track Your Odoo 18 Subscription Payments with Ease

This module provides a robust, professional integration between Odoo 18 Subscriptions and Mollie. It simplifies the complex process of managing recurring payments, mandates, and accounting reconciliation.

Key Features

Renewals Dashboard

Track all upcoming renewals and real-time Mollie payment statuses (Paid, Failed, Pending) in one specialized view.

Automated Mandate Sync

Seamlessly fetches and verifies Mollie mandates upon order confirmation, ensuring recurring billing readiness.

Recurring Charges

Powerful daily background engine that charges active mandates and manages the subscription billing cycle.

Smart Accounting

Automates invoice creation, posting, and reconciliation with successful Mollie payments—reducing manual work.

Payment Recovery

Intelligent handling of failed payments: Odoo payments are automatically reversed if Mollie status fails later.

Safety First

Built-in filters block charges for churned, paused, or closed subscriptions to prevent billing errors.

How It Works

  • 1. Order Confirmation: Once a subscription order is confirmed, the module automatically hooks into Mollie to retrieve or verify the customer's mandate.
  • 2. Automated Billing: A scheduled cron job identifies subscriptions due for renewal, triggers a charge via Mollie API, and generates the corresponding Odoo invoice.
  • 3. Reconcile & Verify: Successful payments automatically reconcile the Odoo invoice. The system continues to monitor the payment status via webhooks and background tasks.
